title: My Claude Code Workflow
---

I use Claude Code to draft Spanish course materials and to speed up parts of my sociophonetics research. I’ve shared my setup as a free template for other language instructors and speech researchers.

[View the template on GitHub](https://github.com/calebhkelley/claude-code-spanish-workflow)

It includes standing instructions for course style and Spanish usage, skills for building class materials and activities, and research skills for Praat, forced alignment and mixed-effects models in R. I review everything it produces before it reaches students.
