# lector-212

Caleb is a sociophonetician teaching SPAN 212, a literature and film course, and wants to get better at literary and film analysis without adding work. The routine rides on planning he already does. He takes the course as a student would, one obra at a time, and his own reading becomes the spine of the class plan.

## When it runs

- At the very start of planning a SPAN 212 class on an obra (Lecciones 4 to 6 this term), before research or building, ask once, in one sentence. Example: «¿Tus tres frases sobre «La siesta del martes»? Sitúa, analiza, conecta, en borrador.»
- Also whenever he says «mi lectura» or pastes a reading of an obra.
- If he says no, later, or no time, go straight to the planning and do not ask again that session. No reminder, no comment.
- If he has not read or watched it yet, offer the short version: the one choice he noticed and what he thinks it does.

## How to answer his reading

Answer in the language he wrote in. Three short parts, about 120 words in all, no headers.

1. What works, named precisely (the move he made, not general praise).
2. One thing he did not see, tied to a specific moment. Quote a line only if you have verified it against the text in this session; otherwise describe the moment and its position in the text.
3. One term he can use in class, with a one-line gloss (for example *focalización*, *elipsis*, *plano contrapicado*, *encabalgamiento*).

Push back the way a literature colleague would. If his reading misstates the plot, say so plainly and give the correct detail. Keep the variationist frame available: what other option did the author have, and what changes because this one was chosen.

House style applies: no em dashes, no colons bridging ideas, no «no es X, es Y» framing, sparing bold, curly apostrophes, no Oxford comma in Spanish.

## Using it in the plan

Build the plan’s model answers and discussion questions from his reading where it holds up, refined by the feedback. Do not mention the routine in any student-facing material.

## Logging it

The notebook is `Planificación/SPAN212_Fall2026_Mi_cuaderno_de_lector_L4-L6.docx` in the SPAN 212 folder. Edit it on his machine with `device_bash` and python-docx, surgically, never by rebuilding (he may have written in it by hand). Back it up to `_to_delete/` with a dated name first.

- Find the obra’s page by its Heading 2 text (compare without «», asterisks or italics).
- Under that page’s Heading 3 labels Sitúa, Analiza and Conecta, insert one paragraph each with his sentence as he wrote it (fix nothing but obvious typos), before the ruled-line table, so the lines stay free for handwriting.
- Replace the «Pendiente.» paragraph in the «Lo que me contestó Claude» panel with your three-part reply.
- If he says what he will take to class, put it under «Lo que me llevo a la clase».
- In the table «El semestre» on page 1, change that obra’s ☐ to ☑.
- Tell him in one short line, for example «Anotado. Van 3 de 11.» Nothing more.

## The instructor ficha

After the plan is built, add a ficha for the obra to `Planificación/SPAN212_Fall2026_Fichas_del_profesor_L4-L6.docx`, following the structure of `SPAN212_Fall2026_Fichas_del_profesor_L1-L3.docx` (la trama, lo que dan por sabido el examen y el cuaderno, personajes, la técnica, para discutir, ojo). Create the file from `Planificación/_fuente_fichas_y_lector/fichas.py` if it does not exist yet. Verify plot details against the text or his materials, and mark anything unverifiable with (confírmalo). This is work for you, not for him, and it is what he grades Examen II from.
