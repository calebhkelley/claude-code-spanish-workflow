# Actividad de síntesis · unit assessment + clave/rúbrica

SPAN 201/202 Fall 2026 has no exams. The Actividad de síntesis IS the unit assessment, done in class, on paper, individually, on the last class day of the unit. It is IPA-like but interpretive plus presentational only, with no interpersonal mode, since students work alone. This skill builds two Emory-styled Word documents (each also re-rendered to PDF for Canvas):

1. The student síntesis (SPAN201_UN_Actividad_de_sintesis.docx).
2. The separate clave y rúbrica (SPAN201_UN_Actividad_de_sintesis_CLAVE_y_RUBRICA.docx), the instructor answer key plus the analytic writing rubric.

Both live in the unit’s síntesis folder. **Read `texto-para-estudiantes` before drafting the student sheet.**

## Before you draft, gather the unit’s materials and set the timing budget

- The unit’s En la red / Exploración theme, so the síntesis sits on the same theme students just worked (the síntesis is the class after En la red). Read the En la red materials.
- Contraseña source text to adapt. The Test Bank and the texto_expansion resource for the unit are mineable freely (Caleb does not administer them), but rewrite the reading at the students’ level from the reporting, do not reproduce it verbatim, and cite the source.
- The unit Vocabulario PDFs (Vocabulario N-1, N-2) and the Resumen gramatical PDFs. The grammar sections must test THIS unit’s grammar, not a carried-over one.
- The previous unit’s síntesis, item by item. You are building the next one in a sequence, not a fresh one. See «Vary the format» below.
- The cuaderno of the unit just taught, so the assessment asks in the shapes the class actually practised, and so any scaffold on the sheet matches the scaffold on the page.
- The calendar for the síntesis date and caleb-profile for voice.

Timing budget. A síntesis must fit in at most half of a 75-minute period, and a slow student or one with extended time must still finish in that half. Target about 31 minutes for a typical student, print 35 on the sheet, treat 38 as the ceiling.

The U13 síntesis took most of the full 75 minutes for students without accommodations, which is the evidence that the estimate had been running low. Two things cost the most minutes per point and are the first to go: modelos the student has to read before starting, and items that require writing an explanation in English. Prefer a selected-response or short-answer version of the same construct. Keep the presentational short so the interpretive and grammar sections carry the weight.

## Vary the format across units

Caleb’s standing correction: an item type that appeared in the last unit’s síntesis does not reappear in this one. Repeating the format turns the assessment into a familiar routine and stops telling him anything new, and he flagged it on two sections at once, vocabulary and grammar-in-the-text.

Keep the construct, rotate the task. Interpretive item types to rotate among:

- Idea principal, four options, one clearly best.
- Cierto / falso con corrección, the correction is what carries the points.
- Vocabulary: definition-to-word in the text · meaning from context (the word inside its own sentence from the text, three paraphrases) · word applied to new short descriptions from a scrambled bank.
- Inferencia con evidencia, answer plus the line that supports it.
- Propósito del autor, what the writer is doing in a named paragraph.
- Organización, match paragraphs to their function, with one distractor left over.
- Detalles de apoyo, which two details support a stated claim.
- Escuchar, short descriptions read aloud twice, a two-column note card.

The grammar section rotates the same way: narrative cloze · choose-and-justify · a scaffolded two-step · sentence completion from a cue. And do not lean on cloze and huecos for the whole grammar section. Caleb’s words: vary the format, use standard ACTFL question types.

## Structure · the 50-point frame

The total is 50 and the sheet fits half a period. The split is set per unit by what the unit actually teaches, not carried over.

- U13 ran 26 / 14 / 10, Interpretación, Gramática en contexto, Expresión escrita.
- U14 ran 8 / 20 / 14 / 8 with a listening section first, because U14 has no written text anywhere: its whole Estrategia de comprensión is *Escuchar para comprender*, its unit text is a video, and a reading-only síntesis would have assessed the unit in the one mode it never taught.

When you change the split, say why in the clave, in a box of its own.

The interpretive ladder is what stays constant: main idea → true/false with correction → vocabulary → inference with evidence → one higher-order item (purpose, organization, or the grammar seen from inside). Five rungs, at whatever point values the unit’s split allows.

A listening section, when the unit needs one, does not need audio: Caleb reads four short descriptions aloud, twice each, and students fill a two-column note card. It needs no images, cannot fail on a speaker, and assesses the unit’s vocabulary productively.

## Modelos · one at most

The old default was a MODELO box under most items. Caleb cut five of six.

Keep a modelo only where the response format is genuinely new and the student cannot otherwise tell how much to write, the two-column listening card is the usual survivor. Everywhere else the consigna already says what the modelo would, and the modelo costs reading minutes the budget does not have. There is never a modelo under a multiple-choice item, a matching item, a blank to fill, or a sentence to correct.

When a modelo does stay, the old rule still holds: it must not contain the answer to the item it sits under, and watch for morphological leaks (a modelo reading «donde se exponen» hands over *exposición*).

## Scaffold the grammar, do not ask for it cold

A double blank asked in the open, write *hace* or *hacía*, and then the verb, is two decisions at once with no visible route between them. Caleb’s note: rethink the format, scaffold it.

The shape that works mirrors the cuaderno: first the meaning, then the form. For each item the student marks which situation it is (sigue hoy · pasó una vez · algo lo cortó) and then writes the forms. Score 1 point for the meaning and 1 for the forms, which also makes the clave diagnostic: you can see whether a wrong answer was a meaning error or a morphology error.

Use the same wording and the same labels the cuaderno used that unit. A scaffold the students have never seen is a new task, not a scaffold.

## The reading (the interpretive text)

Five short paragraphs at the students’ level, numbered [1] to [5], on the unit theme, one step to the side of the En la red work, the same relationship Las Patronas had to La Bestia in U13, or Curruchich to the Guatemalan weavers in U14. Recycle a healthy amount of the unit’s active vocabulary through the reading, not just in the vocabulary item. Keep it short enough for the timing budget. Cite the adapted source in a small grey line under the text. Verify the facts against two independent sources before drafting.

## The vocabulary item, the thing to get right

It must test the UNIT’s active vocabulary, not generic words. The failure mode is «find the word that means X» items whose answers are ordinary words (techo, regalar) rather than unit terms.

- Pick four items from the unit’s active Vocabulario, spread across both halves of the unit.
- Weave those exact words into the reading so they are genuinely present.
- Then pick the task type that did not run last unit. Meaning-from-context shows the word inside its own sentence from the text, the target in bold, with three paraphrases; it assesses understanding the word where it stands rather than recalling a list.
- Name the four targets in the clave so the En la red handout does not reuse them and become a preview of the assessment.

## Glosses for the intermediate-low level

Directions are in Spanish with a brief English gloss in parentheses for task mechanics only (how many, how long, "answer in English"). In the reading and the grammar sections, gloss generously any NON-unit word that could block comprehension for a third-semester reader (encima → on top, gritaron → shouted, sino → but rather). Never gloss the unit words themselves, those are what the test assesses. Glosses render italic grey; keep them inline right after the word.

## Grammar built to the unit

Read the unit’s Resumen gramatical and build to whatever that unit teaches. Textbook traps to know: Resumen 13-2 wrongly says demonstrative pronouns "always go before the noun" (a pronoun replaces the noun); the U14 animation says *desde que* takes a preterite, which is mostly but not always true. Do not reproduce either.

Where a blank is genuinely arguable, accept either answer if correctly conjugated and say so in the clave. Caleb likes those, so plant exactly one per cloze.

Check every item against the unit’s cuaderno for a reused sentence: an item the class already solved in practice is not an item.

## Activity integrity

Nothing in colour, weight or order may reveal an answer. Scramble any bank so it is not in answer order, and spread correct options across positions rather than clustering them. Keep all multiple-choice options visually identical. Keys live only in the clave, never on the student sheet. The fondo/suceso mark the student makes is a scaffold, not an item: say on the sheet and in the clave that it is not graded.

The code is a gesture, not an ink (retired 17 September): *el fondo* se subraya, *el suceso* se rodea, *hace + tiempo* se enmarca [ ]. Same on the sheet, in the cuaderno and on the board.

## The clave y rúbrica doc

Instructor document. Include:
- A points-and-timing box (the split, the timing estimate, the section dates, the make-up policy: LingroHub within 48 hours, documented university-excused absence only).
- A box saying what changed from the previous unit and why, the split, any item type that rotated, any scaffold added. This is the record Caleb reads when he decides whether it worked.
- A content-warning box when the theme warrants it (migration, family separation, and similar).
- Section-by-section answer keys with scoring notes, partial-credit rules and any "accept either" calls. For a scaffolded item, key the meaning step and the form step separately.
- For the presentational section, an analytic rubric of three small grids: Cumplimiento y contenido, the unit grammar, and vocabulary plus the unit’s second structure. Spelling and punctuation have no separate criterion; they only cost points when they block comprehension, inside Cumplimiento.
- A short «origen de los materiales» note citing the adapted source and the Resumen gramatical / Test Bank the items lean on, plus the line naming the vocabulary En la red must avoid.

## Standing conventions
- Register. Student sheet addresses the student in tú. Caleb speaks vosotros in class but never asks it of students, so the sheet is never in vosotros.
- Dates. One document serves all three sections, so leave the Fecha line blank rather than printing a date. Never invent a date.
- The separator is U+0387, not U+00B7. Normalise your search strings before matching against a generator.
- Writing (Caleb’s preferences): no em dashes; curly apostrophes and « » quotes; no colon used to bridge or introduce; avoid the "no es X, es Y" contrast frame; sparing bold; in Spanish no Oxford comma, in English use it.
- Files live in the unit’s síntesis folder. Keep the student and clave filenames parallel. Superseded versions move to a dated `_A_BORRAR/` subfolder, never deleted.
- `contenido.py` holds the reading, every key and every modelo in one module that both documents import, so the sheet and the clave cannot drift. Change an answer there and rebuild both.

## The instrucciones block

Three lines at most, and no line that repeats a rule the sheet already enforces. What earns its place: work alone; paper dictionary yes, anything electronic no; look at all the sections before starting; and any section that cannot be repeated. «Antes de entregar, comprueba que has contestado todo» does not.

## Pagination

Put a page break before the grammar section and before the presentational one. Without them the fondo/suceso legend splits across a page boundary and a section header strands at a page foot. The presentational section gets its own page, with prompt, checklist and ruled lines together.

## Styling engine

Build with python-docx. Write the module below to `engine.py` and `import` it. It is the validated Emory síntesis system: the document default font is serif (Times New Roman) so the reading reads as serif, while headings, labels and glosses set Calibri explicitly. Blue #012169 headings, gold #F2A900 rules, tinted panels #EEF1F7 with a blue left bar, glosses italic grey #5A6478, bold blue bracket numbers.

```python
# engine.py
from docx import Document
from docx.shared import Pt, Inches, RGBColor
from docx.oxml.ns import qn
from docx.oxml import OxmlElement
from docx.enum.table import WD_TABLE_ALIGNMENT
from docx.enum.text import WD_ALIGN_PARAGRAPH as AL

BLUE="012169"; GOLD="F2A900"; TINT="EEF1F7"; CREAM="FCF6E7"; WARN="FDF1D8"
GREY="5A6478"; INK="141414"; HAIR="D8D2CC"; NOTE="4A5568"

def _sub(el,tag,**a):
    e=OxmlElement(tag)
    for k,v in a.items(): e.set(qn(k.replace("_",":")),v)
    el.append(e); return e

def cell_bg(cell,fill):
    _sub(cell._tc.get_or_add_tcPr(),"w:shd",w_val="clear",w_color="auto",w_fill=fill)

def cell_borders(cell,left=None,bottom=None):
    tcPr=cell._tc.get_or_add_tcPr(); tb=tcPr.find(qn("w:tcBorders"))
    if tb is None: tb=OxmlElement("w:tcBorders"); tcPr.append(tb)
    for side,spec in (("left",left),("bottom",bottom)):
        if spec is None: continue
        sz,color=spec
        e=tb.find(qn("w:"+side))
        if e is None: e=OxmlElement("w:"+side); tb.append(e)
        e.set(qn("w:val"),"single"); e.set(qn("w:sz"),str(sz)); e.set(qn("w:space"),"0"); e.set(qn("w:color"),color)

def no_borders(table):
    b=OxmlElement("w:tblBorders")
    for edge in ("top","left","bottom","right","insideH","insideV"): _sub(b,"w:"+edge,w_val="none")
    table._tbl.tblPr.append(b)

def cant_split(row): _sub(row._tr.get_or_add_trPr(),"w:cantSplit")

def keep(p):
    pPr=p._p.get_or_add_pPr()
    for t in ("w:keepNext","w:keepLines"): _sub(pPr,t,w_val="true")

def space(p,before=None,after=None):
    pf=p.paragraph_format
    if before is not None: pf.space_before=Pt(before)
    if after  is not None: pf.space_after =Pt(after)

def run(p,text,size=11,bold=None,italic=None,color=None,name=None):
    r=p.add_run(text)
    if size is not None: r.font.size=Pt(size)
    if bold is not None: r.bold=bold
    if italic is not None: r.italic=italic
    if name is not None: r.font.name=name
    if color is not None: r.font.color.rgb=RGBColor.from_string(color)
    return r

def new_doc(margins=0.85):
    d=Document(); st=d.styles["Normal"]; st.font.name="Times New Roman"; st.font.size=Pt(11)
    st.font.color.rgb=RGBColor.from_string(INK)
    sec=d.sections[0]
    for m in ("top_margin","bottom_margin","left_margin","right_margin"): setattr(sec,m,Inches(margins))
    return d

def gold_rule(p,size=16):
    pPr=p._p.get_or_add_pPr(); pbdr=pPr.find(qn("w:pBdr"))
    if pbdr is None: pbdr=OxmlElement("w:pBdr"); pPr.append(pbdr)
    b=OxmlElement("w:bottom"); b.set(qn("w:val"),"single"); b.set(qn("w:sz"),str(size))
    b.set(qn("w:space"),"4"); b.set(qn("w:color"),GOLD); pbdr.append(b)

def kicker(d,text):
    p=d.add_paragraph(); space(p,after=1); run(p,text,size=9,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri"); return p
def title(d,text):
    p=d.add_paragraph(); space(p,after=3); run(p,text,size=20,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri"); return p
def subtitle(d,text):
    p=d.add_paragraph(); space(p,after=6); run(p,text,size=12,italic=True,color="3C4657",name="Calibri"); return p
def full_gold_rule(d):
    p=d.add_paragraph(); space(p,before=2,after=6); gold_rule(p,18); return p

def nombre_row(d):
    t=d.add_table(rows=1,cols=3); no_borders(t); t.alignment=WD_TABLE_ALIGNMENT.LEFT
    for i,lab in enumerate(("Nombre","Sección","Fecha")):
        c=t.cell(0,i); c.width=Inches(2.2)
        p=c.paragraphs[0]; run(p,lab+"  ",size=10.5,color=NOTE,name="Calibri"); run(p,"_"*24,size=10.5,color=HAIR,name="Calibri")
    space(d.add_paragraph(),after=2); return t

def instrucciones(d,lines):
    t=d.add_table(rows=1,cols=1); no_borders(t); c=t.cell(0,0); cell_bg(c,TINT); cell_borders(c,left=(24,BLUE)); c.width=Inches(6.6)
    mar=OxmlElement("w:tcMar"); c._tc.get_or_add_tcPr().append(mar)
    for s,v in (("top",90),("bottom",90),("start",150),("end",150)): _sub(mar,"w:"+s,w_w=str(v),w_type="dxa")
    first=True
    for segs in lines:
        p=c.paragraphs[0] if first else c.add_paragraph(); first=False; space(p,after=2)
        for txt,kw in segs: run(p,txt,name="Calibri",size=9.5,**kw)
    return t

def section_header(d,text,points):
    t=d.add_table(rows=1,cols=2); no_borders(t)
    l=t.cell(0,0); l.width=Inches(4.8); r=t.cell(0,1); r.width=Inches(1.8)
    run(l.paragraphs[0],text,size=14,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri"); keep(l.paragraphs[0])
    rp=r.paragraphs[0]; rp.alignment=AL.RIGHT
    run(rp,str(points)+" ",size=11,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri"); run(rp,"puntos",size=9,color=NOTE,name="Calibri")
    full_gold_rule(d); return t

def subheader(d,text,points):
    t=d.add_table(rows=1,cols=2); no_borders(t)
    l=t.cell(0,0); l.width=Inches(4.9); r=t.cell(0,1); r.width=Inches(1.7)
    space(l.paragraphs[0],before=6,after=1); run(l.paragraphs[0],text,size=11.5,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri"); keep(l.paragraphs[0])
    rp=r.paragraphs[0]; rp.alignment=AL.RIGHT; space(rp,before=6); run(rp,str(points)+" puntos",size=9.5,color=NOTE,name="Calibri")
    return t

def _seg_runs(p,segs):
    # segs: ('br','[1]') bold blue bracket, ('body','...') serif, ('gloss','(...)') italic grey
    for kind,txt in segs:
        if kind=="br": run(p,txt,size=8.5,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri")
        elif kind=="gloss": run(p,txt,size=11,italic=True,color=GREY)
        else: run(p,txt,size=11)

def reading_panel(d,head,paras,source):
    t=d.add_table(rows=1,cols=1); no_borders(t); c=t.cell(0,0); cell_bg(c,TINT); cell_borders(c,left=(24,BLUE)); c.width=Inches(6.6)
    mar=OxmlElement("w:tcMar"); c._tc.get_or_add_tcPr().append(mar)
    for s,v in (("top",120),("bottom",120),("start",170),("end",170)): _sub(mar,"w:"+s,w_w=str(v),w_type="dxa")
    hp=c.paragraphs[0]; space(hp,after=4); run(hp,head,size=12.5,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri")
    for segs in paras:
        p=c.add_paragraph(); p.alignment=AL.JUSTIFY; space(p,after=4); _seg_runs(p,segs)
    sp=c.add_paragraph(); space(sp,before=4); run(sp,source,size=8.5,color=GREY,name="Calibri"); return t

def write_space(d,n):
    for _ in range(n):
        p=d.add_paragraph(); space(p,before=7,after=7)
        pbdr=OxmlElement("w:pBdr"); b=OxmlElement("w:bottom")
        b.set(qn("w:val"),"single"); b.set(qn("w:sz"),"6"); b.set(qn("w:space"),"2"); b.set(qn("w:color"),HAIR)
        pbdr.append(b); p._p.get_or_add_pPr().append(pbdr)

def rubric_block(d,title_txt,points,rows):
    # rows: list of (level, descriptor)
    t=d.add_table(rows=1,cols=2); no_borders(t)
    l=t.cell(0,0); l.width=Inches(4.9); r=t.cell(0,1); r.width=Inches(1.7)
    space(l.paragraphs[0],before=6,after=1); run(l.paragraphs[0],title_txt,size=11.5,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri")
    rp=r.paragraphs[0]; rp.alignment=AL.RIGHT; space(rp,before=6); run(rp,str(points)+" puntos",size=9.5,color=NOTE,name="Calibri")
    gt=d.add_table(rows=0,cols=2); gt.style="Table Grid"
    tb=OxmlElement("w:tblBorders")
    for e in ("top","left","bottom","right","insideH","insideV"):
        x=OxmlElement("w:"+e); x.set(qn("w:val"),"single"); x.set(qn("w:sz"),"4"); x.set(qn("w:space"),"0"); x.set(qn("w:color"),HAIR); tb.append(x)
    gt._tbl.tblPr.append(tb)
    for lvl,desc in rows:
        row=gt.add_row(); cant_split(row)
        a=row.cells[0]; a.width=Inches(0.7); cell_bg(a,TINT); run(a.paragraphs[0],lvl,size=10,bold=True,color=BLUE,name="Calibri")
        b=row.cells[1]; b.width=Inches(5.9); run(b.paragraphs[0],desc,size=9.5,name="Calibri")
    return gt
```

Usage sketch: `new_doc()` → `kicker` / `title` / `subtitle` / `full_gold_rule` / `nombre_row` / `instrucciones([...])`; then per section `section_header("Sección B · Interpretación",20)`, `subheader("B1. ...",3)`, ordinary Calibri paragraphs for prompts, `reading_panel(head, [[('br','[1]'),('body','...'),('gloss','(...)')], ...], source)`, `write_space(n)` for handwriting room. In the clave, use `rubric_block(title, points, [(level,desc),...])` for the three presentational grids.

## Deliver and verify

- Work on the machine where the files live, with `device_bash`. `python3` there has python-docx, and `soffice`, `pdftoppm` and `pdftotext` are installed. The generators save to `/mnt/user-data/outputs/…`, which does not exist there; strip that prefix.
- Back up the current live files into a dated `_A_BORRAR/` subfolder before overwriting. Never delete.
- Render both to PDF and look at every page. Aptos and the Emory faces are missing on the VM, so `soffice` repaginates wider than Word: treat its page count as a warning, and note that consecutive ruled write-lines collapse into a single rule in that render while being correct in Word.
- `zip -u` into the connected folder fails; build the source zip in `/tmp` and `cp` it in under a new dated name.

## Checklist

- Points sum to 50 and the split matches what the unit teaches, with the reason recorded in the clave.
- No item type repeated from the previous unit’s síntesis.
- At most one modelo, and only for a genuinely new response format.
- The grammar section is scaffolded meaning-first, in the same words the cuaderno used.
- Reading adapted (not copied) from a cited source, facts verified twice, on the unit’s En la red theme.
- Vocabulary targets are unit words, spread across both halves, genuinely present in the reading, and named in the clave so En la red avoids them.
- Glosses for hard non-unit words; never for unit words.
- Grammar built to THIS unit; textbook errors not reproduced; one deliberately arguable blank, flagged in the clave.
- No item reused from the cuaderno.
- Clave has per-item keys, scoring notes, the changed-this-unit box, the presentational rubric, make-up policy and source note.
- Student sheet in tú, Fecha blank, no leaks, banks scrambled, correct options spread across positions.
- Page breaks before the grammar and presentational sections.
- Rendered to PDF and eyeballed; comfortably inside the timing budget for a good student.
